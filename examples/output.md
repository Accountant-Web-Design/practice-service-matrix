# Example Accountancy / fictional practice service brief

Grouped by service and shared client question. No tax advice or automatic page recommendations.

## Review items

- Bookkeeping: Confirm: Confirm actual software and handover process.
- Bookkeeping: Assign a content owner
- Bookkeeping: An unapproved claim is excluded from the publication brief
- Payroll: Confirm: Confirm responsibilities with the practice.
- Annual accounts: Confirm: Approve professional experience claims.

## Bookkeeping

For: Sole traders, Small companies

- What records do you need from me?

Explain the records and working arrangements the practice agrees with each client.
Included: Agreed routine records and a clear handover.
Excluded: Specialist advice outside the written scope.
Next step: Discuss the business records.

Use the shared explanation where the process is the same.
Included: The agreed bookkeeping brief.
Excluded: Any unagreed additional work.
Next step: Describe the records and existing arrangements.

## Payroll

For: Small companies

- Who sends the changes each period?

Explain responsibility for approved payroll inputs.
Included: The agreed payroll process.
Excluded: Employment advice.
Next step: Discuss the payroll brief.

## Annual accounts

For: Charities

- Does the practice support our organisation type?

The practice must approve its actual service scope.
Included: Only work the practice confirms.
Excluded: Unconfirmed specialist work.
Next step: Ask the practice about scope.
