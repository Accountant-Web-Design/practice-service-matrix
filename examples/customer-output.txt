Example Accountancy Practice: content brief

A focused outline for a website writer or designer.

The clients we want to reach
- Limited companies
- Start-ups

Explain each selected service
- Annual accounts: Explain what you need from the client, how the timetable is agreed and what is included.
- Payroll: Explain the employee information needed, deadlines and how changes are reported.

Working with the practice
- An initial conversation, agreed scope in writing and a named practice contact.
- Next step: Book an introductory conversation

Evidence to prepare
- Add only qualifications and memberships the practice has verified.

Keep the brief useful
- State what is included in a service and when a separate quote is needed.
- Ask the practice to approve the final explanations and any professional claims.
- Use a short initial enquiry form. Request detailed financial information through the practice’s agreed secure process.
