# Try Accountancy Website Content Planner and share what would make it more useful

This free browser tool is for accountants and practice managers. Choose the clients and services that matter to your practice, then create a focused brief for your website content.

[Open the tool](https://accountant-web-design.github.io/practice-service-matrix/). No coding knowledge, installation or account is needed. Start with the fictional example, or fill in the labelled fields with your own information. A service-led content brief, with useful questions to answer before writing.

Feedback about unclear questions, missing practical options or mobile usability is welcome here. Please use fictional information in public examples and keep customer or confidential details out of this discussion.

This is a website planning tool. It does not provide tax or accountancy advice, or invent credentials for your practice.

Maintained by [Accountant Web Design](https://accountantwebdesign.uk/), operated by JW Digital Services Ltd. The original developer helpers are still available in the repository, but they are not needed to use the browser tool.
