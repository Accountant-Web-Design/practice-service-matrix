A few useful details

Do I need to know anything about code?

No. Use the labelled fields and the main button. Your result is readable text or a preview, ready to copy, download or print.

Where do my answers go?

Your answers are processed in this browser tab. The tool does not upload, save or email them. Closing or refreshing the page clears them. Downloads and printouts are saved by you. GitHub provides the hosting and handles normal request logs.

Can I keep working on it later?

Download or copy your result before closing the page. This tool does not save a draft or automatically publish anything to your website.
